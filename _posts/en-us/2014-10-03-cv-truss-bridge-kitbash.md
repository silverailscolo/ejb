On the High Line, the train runs just before Silverton over a Pratt Truss steel truss bridge.

With some modifications I kitbashed it from on a [Central Valley #1810 150' Truss Bridge](https://cvmw.com/1800/1810.htm) injection-molded styrene kit. Shorten the bridge from 5 to 4 trusses and customise the shape of the end portals as in the prototype.

## Assembly

- Place the joists on a flat worktop
- Glue the 4 trusses to the joists, checking they are square
- Position the sleeper deck and glue in place

<div class="row">
<div class="col-sm">

- Place the bridge on one side and stick the lower pull rod and the diagonals from thin styrene strip
- Reverse the bridge and complete the other side

## Painting

-
- Spray a coat of primer on the whole bridge using an airbrush
- Let dry well
- Spray with matte gray and rusty brown where you expect rust: horizontal parts, rivets, everywhere!
